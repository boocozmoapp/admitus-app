Features

Everything in One Workspace

Built specifically for international graduate applicants managing complex, multi-country pipelines.

radar

AI Program Discovery

Search universities globally with natural language. Results stream in real-time with personalized fit scores — hover any score to see a 6-dimension breakdown: country, degree, language test, GPA, budget, and scholarship availability.

manage_accounts

Supervisor Intelligence

Track potential supervisors and research professors. Our AI computes a research match score based on your field of study vs. their published interests. Log contact history, schedule follow-ups, and add supervisors directly from faculty search.

view_kanban

Pipeline & Deadlines

Visualize every application from Researching → Preparing → Submitted → Decision with a Kanban board. Deadlines are color-coded by urgency: overdue in red, due within 7 days in amber, safe in green. Never lose track.

link

Quick Add from URL

Paste any university program URL — GradPilot fetches the page, strips it down, and sends it to an LLM that extracts deadline, tuition, IELTS requirement, GPA minimum, and more. Review the extracted details, then save in one click.

smart_toy

AI Program Chat

Open a chat window on any program and ask anything — research focus, admission tips, career outcomes, funding. The bot is grounded in the program's live page, so answers are specific, not generic. Ask about faculty, curriculum, or visa processes.

mark_email_unread

AI Email Triage

Paste any admissions email and AI extracts the deadline, required action, university name, sentiment (positive / neutral / negative), and email type — admission, rejection, interview invite, or document request. Logged to your email history instantly.

folder_managed

Document Vault

Store versioned SOPs, CVs, transcripts, and LORs in one place. Mark items as Draft or Final. Link documents to specific applications so you always know what's submitted where.

switch_account

Multi-Profile Management

Applying to CS in Canada and Policy in Europe simultaneously? Create separate profiles with different country targets, score requirements, and budgets. Fit scores across the app update automatically when you switch.
